# Five Star Institute — Digital Estate Review

A working review surface. Published for comment, not as a finished report.

**Draft 1 — captured 10 August 2026.**

## What this is

An externally-observable map of the Five Star Institute digital estate: 19 digital
properties, 14 platforms, 13 social channels, and an on-page SEO pass across 24 pages
(one per page type per site).

Everything in it was derived from publicly available evidence — page source, DNS records,
tracking identifiers, robots.txt and sitemaps, and public professional profiles. **No
account, portal or system access was used.**

## Reviewing it

Append `?review=1` to the URL, or use the button in the corner. Click any element to
leave a comment. Comments are stored in your own browser only — they are not shared
between reviewers and are not sent anywhere.

## Status and accuracy

This is a draft built to be argued with. Findings resting on a single source are marked
**unverified** in place. Counts and classifications are proposals, not conclusions, and
several open questions are listed in the final section.

If something here is wrong, the review mode is the fastest way to say so.
